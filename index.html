LapDelta Dash · USB flasher

For a dash that will not start, or a brand-new board. Normal updates install over Wi-Fi from the dash's own page; you only need this with a USB cable.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop (Safari and phones cannot talk to USB).
  2. Plug the dash into the laptop with a USB-C data cable.
  3. If the dash does not appear in the port list, hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then try again.
  4. Press Install, pick the port, and leave it alone until it says done. Settings on the dash are kept unless you tick "erase".
This browser cannot flash over USB. Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop. Open this page over HTTPS to flash.
